Springfield gutter FAQs

How often should gutters be cleaned in Western Mass?

Most homes need cleaning at least twice a year — late spring and after fall leaves. Homes under heavy tree cover may need more.

What does gutter cleaning usually cost?

It varies by home size, stories, and debris level. Call for a local estimate rather than relying on national averages.

Do clogged gutters cause ice dams?

They can contribute. Poor drainage and roof/attic heat are also factors. Keeping gutters clear is one practical step.

Seamless vs sectional gutters?

Seamless systems have fewer joints (fewer leak points). Sectional can be easier to repair in spots. A local installer can recommend based on your roof line.
